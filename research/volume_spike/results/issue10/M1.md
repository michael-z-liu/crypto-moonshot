# Issue #10, M1: training harness and the base3 baseline

Plan: `docs/plans/cnn-improvements.md`. All numbers are 2022-24. 2025-26 is computed by the
scorecard but written only to the gitignored `sealed/` folder.

## The harness

`research/volume_spike/i10_train.py` trains m12's path models walk-forward (yearly folds
2022..2026: train before Y-1 July, early-stop on Y-1 H2, score Y). It reuses m12's `Data`,
networks (`make`), `predict`, `loss_fn`, `folds_of`, `PATH_STEPS` and `QUANTILES`, and leaves
m12 unchanged. A config is an `I10Cfg`: an m12 `Cfg` plus these options (defaults in bold
reproduce m12's path_all training):

| Option | Values |
|---|---|
| `select` | **`corr`** (valid corr of the 1h median with y1h, patience 2, at most 6 epochs); `top` (mean net y1h of the top 2% of validation rows by score, same patience); `fixed` (exactly `fixed_epochs`, last kept) |
| `weight` | **`none`**; `day` (equal total weight per UTC day, mean 1); `tail` (1 + 3 x m12's crash mask); `recency` (0.5 ** (age / 1 year) back from the train cut). Batch loss sum(w l) / sum(w), l the row's quantile loss |
| `window_years` | **None** (expanding); N (rows in [cut - N years, cut)) |
| `norm` | **`frozen`** (channel stats from every 10th row before 2021-07, scalars on those rows); `fold` (the same from the fold's own training rows) |
| `volscale` | **off**; on: price channels 0-3 and the path target divided by the row's trailing 5m vol (floor 0.1%), outputs converted back to percent before heads and percentiles; scaled path and head also saved |
| `target_offset` | **None**; a local `cache_all/*.npy` (rows x 32 steps, percent) subtracted from the target (hook for M4) |

Heads: `h1` 1h median (m12's score), `h2` 2h median, `h2ra` 2h median / max(q90 - q10 at 2h,
0.05 pp). Each is saved as a fold percentile (searchsorted against the fold's reference training
rows), for the seed mean and per seed, in `cache_all/wf_i10/<name>.npz`, with a per-epoch log
(`<name>_log.csv`). The module docstring lists exactly what volscale scales.

Scoring: `i10_score.py` scores a head with S5's `a_1h` rule (pct >= 0.98, `CashCapped(5)`, 1h
hold, fee 0.1% + slippage 0.05% + latency 0.05% per side) into this folder's `ledger.csv`
(20 placebos, card in `cards/`), and each seed's own percentiles into `seeds.csv` (not ledger
trials). `scorecard.configure()` points the scorecard at `results/issue10/`; the robustness
ledger is untouched.

## Checks

- **Reproduction.** base3's `pct_seed[0]` and `pct_seed[1]` equal `cache_all/wf/path_all.npz`'s
  on all 1,456,656 scored rows (2022-26): max abs diff 0, no row differs. The per-epoch valid
  correlations of seeds 0-1 equal path_all's log on all 45 (fold, seed, epoch) rows. MPS
  training is deterministic here, so no tolerance is needed. (A first smoke run of fold 2022,
  seed 0 matched as well.)
- **Options** (`i10_train.py selfcheck`): day weights give every day the same total and mean 1
  (synthetic and on the real 2024 fold's training rows, expanding and 3-year window); recency is
  1 at the cut, 0.5 one half-life back; tail is 1 + k on crash rows; unit weights give m12's
  loss; the heads, the h2ra floor, the vol round trip, the vol of log returns and the top-2% mean
  are checked; training rows never pass the cut.
- **Smoke runs** (fold 2022, seed 0, every 4th training row, 3 epochs; per-seed pct compared with
  the same run at defaults on 296,060 rows): `fixed`, `day`, `tail`, `recency`,
  `window_years=1`, `norm=fold`, `volscale`, `target_offset` (a constant 0.5 pp test file) each
  change 99.6-100% of the rows (max abs pct diff 0.24-1.0). `select=top` picked the same epoch as
  corr on fold 2022, so it was also run on fold 2023 (same settings, up to 6 epochs): it kept
  epoch 0 and stopped after 3 epochs where corr kept epoch 1 after 4, changing 99.98% of rows.

Seed-mean day-bootstrap 95% CI [+31.2, +207.1] bp; without the 5 best days +56.8 bp. For
reference, the 2-seed `S5:path_all:a_1h` (seeds 0-1, robustness ledger) is +147.1 / +206.1 /
+62.1, mean +138.4, not fragile (2022 w/o best trade +16.8).

**Noise band** (max - min of the per-seed 2022-24 mean bp): 129.4 - 102.6 = **26.8 bp**. A
trained variant beats base3 only if it qualifies, is not fragile and its seed-mean mean exceeds
+120.9 + 26.8 = +147.7 bp.

The base3 seed mean qualifies but is **fragile**: 2022 turns to -19.6 bp per trade without its
single best trade. Adding seed 2 lowered the mean from the 2-seed +138.4 to +120.9 (seed 2 takes
641 trades in 2024 at +13.8 bp).

epochs run / kept:

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2024 | 830,148 | 2.2 (5 / 3) | 1.3 (3 / 1) | 1.7 (4 / 2) |
| 2025 | 1,193,924 | 3.6 (6 / 5) | 3.6 (6 / 5) | 2.4 (4 / 2) |
| 2026 | 1,549,696 | 3.8 (5 / 3) | 2.2 (3 / 1) | 3.0 (4 / 2) |

29.7 minutes in total for 5 folds x 3 seeds (about 10 minutes per seed). The volatility
array for `volscale` (one pass over seq2) is cached in `cache_all/wf_i10/vol_s96.npy`.
