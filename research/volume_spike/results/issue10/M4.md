# Issue #10, M4: target, scaling and stationarity (ideas 5, 6, 8)

Plan: `docs/plans/cnn-improvements.md`. All numbers are 2022-24; 2025-26 is only in the gitignored
`sealed/` folder. Each variant is the path_all base config (3 seeds) changing one option, trained
with `i10_train.py run <name>` (driver `cache_all/wf_i10/m4_driver.sh`), then scored (h1 head,
`a_1h` rule, 20 placebos) with `i10_score.py score <name> --heads h1 --stage M4`.

| Variant | Idea | Change |
|---|---|---|
| `xs_univ` | 5 | target minus the universe's equal-weight forward return over the same window (`target_offset="wf_i10/univ_fwd.npy"`) |
| `volscale` | 6 | price channels 0-3 and the target divided by the row's trailing 96-candle std of 5m log returns (floor 0.001); outputs multiplied back before ranking |
| `recency` | 8 | loss weight 0.5 ** (age in years / 1.0), age back from the fold's train cut |
| `roll3y` | 8 | train only on the 3 years before the train cut; identical to base3 for folds 2022-23 (cuts before 2023-01) |
| `foldnorm` | 8 | channel and scalar normalisation statistics from the fold's own training rows (instead of frozen pre-2021-07) |

## The universe forward return (`i10_univ.py`)

- The path target is `fwd2[:, 2, k] = close[e+k] / open[e] - 1` (e = the entry bar, the candle
  after the spike, on the coin's gap-free grid), clipped net of 0.3% to +-30 pp, in percent.
- The offset for a row is `100 * mean_c clip(close_c[e+k] / open_c[e] - 1, +-0.3)` over the 582
  "all" coins (pegged coins and stocks excluded, the row's own coin included), each on the same grid
  construction; a coin counts at a step only if its grid covers the entry bar and that step. No
  cost: target minus offset is the coin's excess return net of its own round trip.
- It is a forward *label*, never a model input.
- Checks (`i10_univ.py check`, all passed):
  1. 6 random rows recomputed from the raw month files with independent code: all 32 steps match
     (max abs diff 2.4e-7), 195 to 426 coins per row.
  2. The builder's own-coin returns equal `fwd2` on every row of 40 random symbols (4,994,884
     values, max abs diff 2.4e-7, none off by more than 1e-6).
  3. Coverage: median coins per row at 1h 135 / 247 / 319 / 340 / 372 / 391 / 410 for 2020..2026;
     no finite target has a NaN offset; 0.06% of coin returns clipped. At 1h the target sd is 3.51
     pp, the offset sd 1.45 pp, their correlation 0.443.
  4. Look-ahead: altering every candle outside [entry bar, window end] for 5 rows x 40 coins changed
     nothing.

- **volscale** is the strongest change of the study so far: +223.6 bp seed-mean, every year above
  base3, not fragile, +192.9 at 0.6% and +111.9 one candle late, and two of three seeds far above
  any base3 seed. Its third seed (+112.2) is base3-like, so the seed spread is large (84 bp).
- **foldnorm** beats the bar (+170.8), but per seed it is only ~20 bp above base3: most of its gain
  is in the seed-mean book.
- **xs_univ** fails: the excess-return target trades 2-3x as often at a fraction of the edge, in
  every seed. Caveat: it early-stopped on the correlation of the (now excess-return) h1 forecast
  with raw y1h, the default.
- **recency** lifts 2023-24 but is fragile (2022 without its best trade -47.9).
- **roll3y** equals base3 in 2022-23 by construction; 2024 is +51.1 vs +48.0.
- No variant narrows the seed spread below base3's 26.8 bp.
