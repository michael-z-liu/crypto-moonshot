# Issue #10, M2: cascade caps and score heads, no training

Plan: `docs/plans/cnn-improvements.md`. All numbers are 2022-24 (2025-26 is written only to the
gitignored `sealed/` folder and was not read). Scores: base3 (path_all, 3 seeds). Code:
`research/volume_spike/i10_m2.py`; ledger rows `M2:base3:<rule>` (20 placebos) in `ledger.csv`,
per-seed rows (3 each) in `seeds.csv`, cards in `cards/M2_*`.

## Definitions

- **Candidate**: a scored row with pct >= 0.98 within [2022-01-01, 2026-11-02).
- **Cascade**: a maximal run of candidates, across all symbols, sorted by open time, in which
  consecutive candidates are at most 60 minutes apart. Built from candidates only; every seed has
  its own percentiles and so its own cascades.
- **Cascade age** of a candidate: minutes since its cascade's first candidate.
- Every rule is S5's `a_1h` (pct >= 0.98, `CashCapped(5)`, highest score first, 1h hold, fee 0.1%
  + slippage 0.05% + latency 0.05% per side) except for the change named below.

## Rules

| Rule | Change |
|---|---|
| `daycap5`, `daycap10` | at most 5 / 10 entries per UTC day, first come first served (`risk.MaxEntriesPerDay`) |
| `casc5`, `casc10` | at most 5 / 10 entries per cascade (new engine `max_entries_per_group`) |
| `dll3`, `dll6` | `risk.DailyLossLimit`: no new entries for the rest of the UTC day once the day's **realised** P&L has fallen to 3% / 6% of the day's starting realised equity. Open positions are not marked to market, so a position that is down but still open does not count until it exits; once hit, the halt lasts to midnight UTC |
| `age60`, `age180` | only candidates with cascade age <= 60 / 180 minutes (a row filter) |
| `esc` | entries 1-5 of a cascade need pct >= 0.98, entries 6-10 need >= 0.99, later ones >= 0.995 (new engine `group_tiers=((5, 0.99), (10, 0.995))`; it counts the entries the book took, not the signals offered) |
| `h2_1h`, `h2_2h` | pct_h2 (2h median head), 1h / 2h hold |
| `h2ra_1h`, `h2ra_2h` | pct_h2ra (2h median / (q90 - q10)), 1h / 2h hold |
| `h1_2h` | the h1 head with a 2h hold (reference for the 2h rows) |

### Read the per-seed columns before trusting these

The seed-mean score is a different (smoother) ranking than any single seed's, and the base rule's
seed-mean row (+120.9) sits near its per-seed average (+114.0). Against that per-seed average:

- `esc` is the one rule whose gain shows in every seed (+154.8 / +125.0 / +163.2 vs base seeds
  +110.1 / +102.6 / +129.4; average +147.7, +33.7).
- `h1_2h` (hold alone) improves every seed too (+160.2 / +158.4 / +144.0, average +154.2).
- `daycap5` does not hold up: its per-seed average is +106.4, below base's +114.0, although the
  seed-mean row says +172.9 (155 / 86 / 214 trades, 2023 on 86 trades).
- `h2_1h` per-seed average +115.5 (no gain over base); `h2_2h` per-seed average +140.0 is below
  `h1_2h`'s +154.2, so the 2h-median head is not better than the 1h-median head at a 2h hold on
  average over seeds, even though its seed-mean row is the highest. `h2ra_*` is clearly worse
  (per-seed averages +23.7 and +48.2).
- Cascade caps at 5, daily loss limits and the day cap at 10 all lower the seed-mean and
  per-seed means (the reason is not analysed here).

## Implementation notes and deviations

- **Engine change (needed):** `BacktestConfig` gained `max_entries_per_group` and `group_tiers`
  (signal columns `group`, `score`), with skip reasons `group_cap` and `group_tier`. Defaults are
  `None`, which changes nothing; pytest tests added in `tests/test_backtest_engine.py`. A risk
  check cannot do this: `RiskCheck.allows(book, symbol, stake)` sees neither the signal's group nor
  its score. The scorecard's `Rule` got the fields `daily_loss_limit`, `max_per_group`,
  `group_tiers` (described in the config hash only when set) and `scorecard(signal_cols=...)`.
  `dll` uses the engine's existing `daily_loss_limit` unchanged.
- **Placebos:** the scorecard shuffles scores within each UTC day among the rows that pass the row
  filter. For `age60` / `age180` the row filter is True for every row except candidates older than
  the limit, computed once from the real scores, so a placebo draw has as many candidates as the
  real book had survivors. Priority is the default (best shuffled score first). For `casc*` and
  `esc` the `group` and `score` columns are recomputed from each draw's own shuffled scores, so a
  placebo's cascades are those of its own random candidates. Day caps, loss limits and the caps
  are applied as in the real book. A placebo's cascades are mostly small, so the cascade rules
  bind less on placebos than on the real book.
- Cascade statistics cover 2022-24 only; the cascade ids themselves were built over all years
  (candidates only, no results).
- No rule was added or changed after seeing results; the grid is the fourteen above.
