# Issue #10, M3: objective and sampling (ideas 1-2)

Plan: `docs/plans/cnn-improvements.md`. All numbers are 2022-24; 2025-26 is only in the gitignored
`sealed/` folder. Each variant is the path_all base config (3 seeds) changing only the options
named, trained with `i10_train.py run <name>`, then scored (h1 head, `a_1h` rule, 20 placebos)
with `i10_score.py score <name> --heads h1 --stage M3`.

| Variant | Change |
|---|---|
| `es_top` | `select=top`: early stop on the mean 1h net return of the top 2% of validation rows by the h1 score (patience 2) |
| `tail` | `weight=tail` (1 + 3 x crash mask) plus `select=top` |
| `day` | `weight=day`: each UTC day carries equal total loss weight; early stop on corr |
| `fixed` | `select=fixed`, `fixed_epochs=2`, no early stop, last epoch kept |

## The fixed epoch count N = 2

N is the median over base3's nine (fold, seed) runs in folds 2022-24 of (kept epoch + 1), where the
kept epoch is the one with the best `valid_corr` among the epochs run (0-indexed). The values are
2022: 1, 1, 2; 2023: 3, 5, 3; 2024: 3, 1, 2. Sorted: 1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3, 5; the median is
**2**. Folds 2025-26 were not used.

## Seed spread and the verdict

| Variant | Per-seed mean bp | Spread (max - min) | vs 26.8 | Seed-mean mean | Qualifies | Fragile | > +147.7 | Beats base3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| base3 | +110.1 / +102.6 / +129.4 | 26.8 | reference | +120.9 | yes | yes | no | (reference) |
| es_top | +125.4 / +151.7 / +121.6 | 30.1 | wider | +152.8 | yes | **yes** | yes | **no** (fragile) |
| tail | +125.8 / +139.3 / +102.4 | 36.9 | wider | +136.8 | yes | no | no | **no** (below +147.7) |
| day | +135.3 / +114.6 / +149.4 | 34.8 | wider | +178.7 | yes | no | yes | **yes** |
| fixed | +102.6 / +165.8 / +125.4 | 63.2 | much wider | +139.0 | yes | **yes** | no | **no** |

**Idea 2's question (does the variant reduce seed noise?)** No. `day` has a larger per-seed spread
(34.8 bp) than base3 (26.8 bp), and so do all the others. Two of the three `day` seeds are fragile
on their own, as in base3. Its seed-mean gain over base3 (+57.8 bp) is much larger than the spread,
mostly from 2022 (+285 vs +106 bp, on 156 trades vs 289), while 2024 is similar (+42 vs +48). A
seed-mean 2022 book of 156 trades is thin, so the +178.7 bp rests on few trades; the day-bootstrap
CI is [+69.2, +277.8] and the mean without the 5 best days is +88.0 (base3 +56.8).

**Idea 1.** `es_top` raises the seed mean to +152.8 but 2022 turns negative (-29.7) when the best
trade is removed, so it is fragile and fails the protocol. Its per-seed results equal base3's in
folds where the top-2% metric picked the same epoch (2022 seeds 0-1: identical bp). `tail` is not
fragile but at +136.8 is within the noise band of base3. `fixed` at 2 epochs gives the widest seed
spread (63.2 bp); removing the early stop made seeds less alike, not more.

**Verdict under the protocol:** only `day` beats the baseline. `es_top`, `tail` and `fixed` do not.

## Training time

5 folds x 3 seeds each, Apple M4 (MPS), one training at a time: base3 29.7 min (M1), es_top 32.7
min, tail 32.9 min, day 26.4 min, fixed 13.9 min. Total for the four variants: 105.9 min. Each is
longer or shorter mainly by the number of epochs run.

Reproduce:

    cd research/volume_spike && export MOONSHOT_UNIVERSE=all
    for v in es_top tail day fixed; do
      uv run --group research python i10_train.py run $v
      uv run --group research python i10_score.py score $v --heads h1 --stage M3
    done
