## Surprising or notable

- **The pick is the weakest of the top three out of sample.** `volday5` had the best 2022-24 numbers
  under every eligible rule, but its 2025 is poor (a +13, b +42, c -6, d -23 bp), while the runner-up
  `volscale5:b_h1_2h` makes +120 in 2025 and +125 over 2025-26 with a CI above zero. Combining
  volscale and day weights did not carry over: per seed, `volday5` rows are positive (4-5 of 5 seeds)
  but small, and its 2025-10-10 losses are as large as volscale's.
- **Day weighting is the most robust change out of sample.** Every `day5` rule but `esc_2h` (4 of 5) is positive in 2025-26
  in all five seeds (2025 bp +69..+107 for the seed mean), with the smallest drawdowns (-9% to -18%)
  and the largest $ P&L (`day5:esc` +104% over 2025-26, `day5:a_h1_1h` +78%). Its 2025-10-10 loss is
  -2k..-4k bp against -9k..-11k for the `volscale5` and `volday5` 1h rules. `M3:day:h1` (+90) also beats base3 (+46).
- **Volatility scaling alone does not survive at 1h.** `M4:volscale:h1` (3 seeds, the M4 winner at
  +223.6) makes +19 over 2025-26 (2025 +9); with 5 seeds the 1h book makes +60. Its gain comes back
  with the 2h hold (`volscale5:b` +125, `c` +120), which turns the 2025-10-10 day from -11k to +3k..+4k
  bp; for base3 the same switch (M2 `h1_2h`) turns -3.3k into +14.2k.
- **2h beats 1h in 2025**, as in 2022-24: averaged over the three score sets, 2025 bp +71 at 2h vs +34
  at 1h ((b)+(c) vs (a)+(d)), and +84 vs +61 over 2025-26. The exception is `esc`, where 1h and 2h tie.
- **The escalating threshold (`esc`) is the best rule out of sample** on two of three score sets
  (`day5:esc` +131, `volday5:esc` +95 vs the pick +64), and gains on base3 too (+60 vs +46). It was a
  comparison row by the pre-registered decision, so it could not be picked.
- **2022-24 ranks the broad field but not the finalists.** Spearman +0.62 over all 42 #10 rows (the
  failed ideas, e.g. `xs_univ`, `h2ra`, loss limits, are poor in both periods) but +0.04 over the 18 M5
  rows: among strong candidates the 2022-24 ordering says nothing about 2025.
- **Seed mean vs per seed.** For several M5 rows the seed-mean 2025-26 is far above the seed average
  (`volscale5:c_h2_2h` +120 vs +1.6, `volscale5:b` +125 vs +39.5; `M2:base3:h2_2h` +113 vs +38.6);

  (+27 / +7 / +71 / +20 / +59, avg +36.7), so its sign holds per seed but its size is uncertain.
- The daily loss limits (`dll3`, `dll6`) are the worst M2 rules out of sample (-1 and +15) and both

  while the day's first losing positions are still open (a likely reading, not checked trade by trade).
