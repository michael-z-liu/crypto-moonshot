# S4: inputs (CNN robustness study)

Plan: `docs/plans/cnn-robustness.md`. Code: `build_s4_inputs.py` (the datasets, re-runnable:
`ctx`, `h1`, `m1`), `s4_inputs.py` (`chain`, `train`, `score`, `table`, `timings`), and an
architecture registry in `m12_cnn_wf.py` (`ARCHS`; the built-in archs are unchanged). Ledger:
`ledger.csv`, variants `S4:<name>`; cards in `cards/S4_*`; tables in `S4_table.csv`,
`S4_timings.csv`. Only 2022-24 is shown; 2025-26 stays sealed.

## Setup

The four variants were fixed before any result. Each is **path_all** (all 13 seq2 channels, 8h
path quantile forecast, scored by the 1h median, 2 seeds, 6 epochs with early stop, yearly
walk-forward: fold Y trains before Y-1 July, early-stops on Y-1 H2) plus new inputs, scored with
the default rule (pct >= 0.98, 5 equal slots, 1h hold, 0.1% + 0.05% per side, continuous
production-engine book). Every input uses data up to the close of the spike candle only.

- **ctx**: path_all's `liq` (log qv_base) plus m12's `age` (log1p listed_days) plus 7 context
  scalars from `s4_ctx.parquet`: log tick_rel (events.parquet via `event`), btc_ret_4h, log
  btc_vol_mult, log1p of the distinct coins with a spike candidate (a seq2_meta row) with open
  time in [t - 55m, t] (the 12 candles ending with this one) across the universe, log1p of those
  coins with a candidate in the window at ret_4h <= -10%, the row's ret_4h rank among the
  candidates of the same open time (0 = deepest drop, 1 = highest, 0.5 when alone) and
  spike_share (market.parquet). Each new scalar is z-scored on rows before 2021-07 like m12's,
  NaN -> 0, clipped to +-10. m12's own scalars are unchanged (checked: `liq` identical).
- **long7d**: a second conv branch on `seq_1h7d.npy`, 8 channels x 168 one-hour bars: coin OHLC
  as log ratio to the spike close, log1p(hour volume / median of the 168 bars), taker-buy share
  - 0.5, BTC close as log ratio to BTC at the spike, BTC log1p(volume / its 168-bar median).
  **Partial-hour handling:** bars are anchored at the spike close, not at clock hours. The last
  bar is the 12 5m candles ending with the spike candle (the 55 minutes before it plus the spike
  candle), each earlier bar steps back 12 candles, so every bar is a full hour and none reaches
  past the spike close. Built from `build_seq2.grid` (missing 5m candles carry the close with
  zero volume). Every seq2 row has listed_days >= 7, so all rows have 168 full bars.
- **m1last**: a second conv branch on `seq_1m60.npy`, 7 channels x 60 one-minute candles with
  open times t - 55m .. t + 4m (t = the spike candle's open; the t + 4m close is the spike
  close): coin OHLC as log ratio to that close, log1p(volume / median of the present minutes),
  taker share - 0.5, and a mask (1 = minute present). Missing minutes are zeros with mask 0; a row
  without the t + 4m minute is all zeros.
- **inputs_all**: ctx + both branches.
- **Network:** m12's CNN trunk on seq2 unchanged; each branch is three conv blocks (32 filters,
  kernels 5 / 5 / 3, one max-pool) whose mean, max and last step (96 numbers) join the trunk's
  features and the scalars before m12's head. Branch inputs are z-scored per channel with the
  mean / sd of every 10th row before 2021-07 (as m12 does for seq2), clipped to +-10.
- Spot checks: three random rows' last two hour bars and taker share equal the raw 5m candles
  (to float16 precision); five random rows' 1m windows aggregate to the same last-hour bar as
  `seq_1h7d.npy`.

## Training

The chain waited for S3 to release the GPU (12:19Z) and trained one variant at a time. Epochs
kept = best validation epoch + 1, per (fold, seed), all 10 fits; validation correlation (1h
median forecast vs y1h on Y-1 H2) is the mean over the 2022-24 folds only (the 2025 / 2026
folds early-stop on 2024 H2 / 2025 H2).

| Variant | Wall time | Epoch time (sum) | Epochs run (mean) | Epochs kept (mean, min-max) | Valid corr 2022-24 folds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| path_all (S0) | 21 min | 19.9 min | 4.5 | 2.8 (1-5) | 0.195 |
| ctx | 19.3 min | 18.6 min | 4.1 | 2.4 (1-6) | 0.194 |
| long7d | 39.1 min | 37.3 min | 3.9 | 2.0 (1-5) | 0.198 |
| m1last | 24.5 min | 23.3 min | 4.1 | 2.2 (1-5) | 0.198 |
| inputs_all | 48.1 min | 45.7 min | 4.0 | 2.1 (1-5) | 0.201 |

No variant was cut. The original deadline (13:30Z, 4.5 h after S4 started) was moved to 15:30Z
by the master after a 3-hour usage-limit pause; the chain ended at 14:31Z. The long7d branch is
slow because of random reads from the 5 GB memory-mapped file next to seq2 (resident memory
about 15 GB).

## Conclusion (2022-24 only)

- **ctx is the one input that helps.** +188 vs +152 mean bp, better on every robustness measure:
  0.6% cost +159 (vs +121), late +111 (vs +89), without the 5 best days +112 (vs +85), CI lower
  bound +97 (vs +69), 2022 without its best trade +93 (vs +27), and about half path_all's
  drawdowns. The gain is in 2022 (+268 vs +162); 2023 (+218) and 2024 (+79) equal path_all.
  For scale, S3's path_all5 (5 seeds, the same config) moves the years by 5-20 bp against
  path_all, so the 2022 gain is larger than seed noise, but it is one year. It ranks first
  among the S4 rows and above path_all and ens3.
- **The longer and finer price inputs hurt.** long7d +140 (2024 +32, +2 at 0.6%), m1last +119
  (fragile: 2022 -28 without its best trade; CI lower bound +6), inputs_all +129 (2024 +48, +8
  late). Their validation correlation is slightly higher (0.198-0.201 vs 0.195), but the top 2%
  they pick trades worse: a better average fit, not a better tail ranking. The extra branches
  also add 3-27 minutes of training. inputs_all does not keep ctx's gain; the branches dominate.
- Every S4 variant qualifies (criteria 1-4), so the verdict does not separate them; the 2024 and
  late columns do.
- For S5: `S4:ctx` is the candidate. Its context features also overlap the S2 gates (BTC 4h
  return, BTC volume, spike share are now inputs), so ctx + a BTC gate is not two independent
  gains. The ledger has 4 new rows (all trials counted).
