# S0: baselines and scorecard (CNN robustness study)

Plan: `docs/plans/cnn-robustness.md`. Code: `scorecard.py` (the scorecard), `s0_baseline.py`
(this stage). Ledger: `ledger.csv`. Cards: `cards/<variant>.txt`. Only 2022-24 is shown here;
2025-26 is computed and sealed in `sealed/<variant>.json` (gitignored, opened only in S5). Note:
base, market and path_all on "all" were already seen for 2025-26 in PR #8.

## Setup

- **Scores:** `m12_cnn_wf.py run base market path_all` with `MOONSHOT_UNIVERSE=all`, the
  existing configs (2 seeds, 6 epochs, early stop on Y-1 H2), into local `cache_all/wf/`. The
  research simulator gives exactly PR #8's 2022-24 numbers for all three (table below), so the
  retraining reproduced PR #8's scores.
- **ens3:** mean of the three `pct` arrays (NaN unless all three are present), traded with the
  same rule (`mean >= 0.98`): 19,282 candidate rows in 2022-24 against 21,111 / 24,634 / 22,027
  for base / market / path_all. No threshold was tuned.
- **Rule (plan default):** candidates `pct >= 0.98`, highest score first, 5 slots,
  `EqualSlots(5)`, 1-hour hold, fee 0.1% + slippage 0.05% per side.
- **Book:** the production engine, one continuous book from 2022-01-01 to the data end
  2026-11-02 (exclusive, as the golden tests), $10,000. Years are entry years.
- **Stress:** 0.6% round trip (fee 0.1% + slippage 0.2% per side); one candle late
  (`entry_delay=1`).
- **Placebo:** 20 draws, scores permuted within each UTC day among that day's scored rows (so
  each day keeps its number of candidates; coin and time within the day are random). Same rule.
  Books 2022-24.
- **CI:** 2,000 bootstrap draws of entry days, within each year; the statistic is the mean of
  the three yearly bp.
- **Without 5 best days:** the 5 entry days of 2022-24 with the largest summed net return
  dropped.
- "Mean bp" is the average of the three yearly bp per trade, the Selection rule's ranking key.
  Criterion 4 is read as: mean bp 2022-24 above the 95th percentile of the 20 placebo draws'
  mean bp.

### What the numbers hide

- **2022 hangs on LUNA.** One-hour trades into the Terra collapse (2022-05-11/12) earned up to
  +36,437 bp (+364%) each. Without its single best 2022 trade, base's 2022 is -67.0 bp, ens3's is
  -14.9 bp; path_all +26.8, market +68.9. So base and ens3 pass criterion 1 thanks to one trade.
  Later stages should look at "without 5 best days" and the CI, not only the verdict.
- **The placebo is a low bar.** Random coins on the same days lose about 80 bp per trade
  (95th percentile -56 to -66). Every CNN beats it by 140+ bp, so criterion 4 does not
  discriminate between CNN variants. Criteria 1-3 are the binding ones.
- **The weak spot is 2024 when one candle late** (+14.6 to +28.4 bp in every variant), and
  market's 2024 at 0.6% (-6.2 bp).

The research column equals PR #8's `results/universe_all/m12_variants.csv` to the decimal
(base 92.8 / 175.5 / 73.8 with 266 / 256 / 411 trades). The differences, by the causes in
`docs/backtest-validation.md`:

- **Research to parity, cost model (b):** the same trades in every year and variant; bp within
  1.0 (-0.9 to +1.0).
- **Parity to per-year, cash check (a):** with equal slots the engine skips an entry it cannot
  pay for. This is -3.1 to +4.9 bp in most years, -12.3 bp in market 2022 (the size of F2's
  2022 in the validation doc), and **-74.0 bp in base 2022**. That is not a bug. On 2022-05-11
  at 13:10 base signals LUNA. Parity takes it with the $1,322 of cash left and makes +36,437 bp
  in one hour. Under `EqualSlots` the stake (about $2,300) exceeds the cash, so the engine skips
  it and takes LUNA at 13:30 instead (+21,715 bp). The slot chain then differs through the Terra
  collapse: 20 research trades are not taken (summed +41,403 bp) and 9 others are (+21,518 bp);
  the common trades are identical. The two LUNA entries alone are 14,722 of the 19,885 bp
  difference in summed returns.
- **Per-year to continuous books (f):** none in 2022-24 except market 2023 / 2024 (one trade
  each, +0.9 / +0.1 bp). The continuous book carries positions and equity over New Year, which
  changes a slot or a cash skip.

## For S1-S4

- **API:** `from scorecard import Rule, scorecard, show, wf_scores`, run from
  `research/volume_spike` with `MOONSHOT_UNIVERSE=all`:
  `card = scorecard(name, scores, Rule(q=..., slots=..., exit=ExitPlan(...), sizing=...,
  costs=..., entry_delay=...), stage="S1", source="...", row_filter=mask, priority=array)`,
  then `print(show(card))`. Arrays align with `seq2_meta.parquet` rows. The call upserts the
  ledger and writes the sealed file. `source` is part of the config hash, so describe the
  scores precisely. Use `ledger=False` for exploratory runs that must not count as trials
  (they should be rare: the ledger is the trial count for the deflated Sharpe).
- **Not covered by `Rule`:** score-weighted sizing (the engine's `Sizing` sees the book, not the
  signal) and caps per crash day. A day cap that ranks a whole day's candidates looks ahead.
  Both need a small engine-side hook or a custom signal pass.
- **Timing on this Mac (M4, 24 GB):** training one 2-seed variant on "all" takes 16 min (base),
  17 min (market, 10 channels) or 21 min (path_all, 13 channels, path head). seq2 is
  memory-mapped (now the default for files over 6 GB). Training's resident memory reached
  13.8 GB, mostly page cache of the mapped file, with no memory pressure. A scorecard runs in
  8-16 s, alongside training.
