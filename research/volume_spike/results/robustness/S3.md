# S3: training and ensembles (CNN robustness study)

Plan: `docs/plans/cnn-robustness.md`. Code: `s3_train.py` (this stage) and two additions to
`m12_cnn_wf.py` (loss `asym`, fold schedule `half`). Ledger rows `S3:<name>`; cards
`cards/S3_<name>.txt`; table `S3_table.csv`; timings `S3_timings.csv`. Only 2022-24 is shown.

## Setup

- **Rule:** the plan's default for every variant (pct >= 0.98, 5 slots, `EqualSlots(5)`, 1h hold,
  0.3% round trip), except the two ensembles, whose threshold matches path_all's 22,027
  candidate rows in 2022-24 at q 0.98. That uses no outcomes.
- **CNNs:** 2 seeds, 6 epochs, early stop on validation correlation with y1h (as S0), unless
  stated. Universe "all".
  - `allch_y1h`: all 13 channels, y1h, MSE. This is the control for `allch_xs`.
  - `allch_xs`: as `allch_y1h` with loss `xs` (target minus the same UTC day's mean). It is
    causal. The day mean enters only the training target. Training rows end at a UTC midnight
    cut, so every training day is complete and in the past. Validation uses raw y1h, and scoring
    reads only the inputs.
  - `allch_cost`: as `allch_y1h` with the new loss `asym`. The target is y1h - 0.3%; the loss is
    the squared error, doubled where the forecast is above the target.
  - `path_all5`: path_all with 5 seeds (m12's existing config; seeds 0-1 are path_all's).
  - `path_all_h6`: path_all retrained every 6 months. Fold H (2022H1..2026H2) trains before
    H - 6 months, early-stops on the 6 months before H and scores H. Percentiles use the fold's
    own training scores.
- **Stackers:** sklearn `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` with fixed parameters chosen before any
  result (200 iterations, lr 0.05, 15 leaves, min leaf 1,000, L2 1.0, no early stopping). The
  target is y1h, clipped to ±20%.
  - `stack_gbm` features: the 8 CNN pct scores (base, market, path_all and the 5 S3 CNNs) plus
    the causal scalars log qv_base, listed_days, hour, ret_4h, ret_24h, btc_vol_mult, btc_ret_4h
    and vol_mult. `gbm_scalars` uses the scalars only.
  - Folds: test year Y is fitted on 2022-01-01..Y-01-01 minus 2h, so no 1h outcome reaches into
    Y. Scores are percentiles of the fold's in-sample training predictions, as m12 does.
  - The CNN scores exist only from 2022, so the stackers start in 2023. **2023-24 only:** 2022 is
    unscored. They cannot meet criterion 1, and their ledger rows carry NaN for 2022 and for the
    2022-24 means.
- **Ensembles:**
  - `ens_s3`: the mean pct of the 8 CNNs. Threshold 0.97534.
  - `ens_top`: the mean pct of the 3 best qualified CNNs of S0+S3 by the 2022-24 ranking key.
    These are path_all5 (+154.2), path_all (+152.0) and path_all_h6 (+149.3). Threshold 0.97834.
    path_all5 contains path_all's two seeds, so this ensemble overweights them.
- **Fragile:** some 2022-24 year is negative without that year's single best trade (computed
  from the card's book).

### Per-variant notes

- **Rank-within-day loss (`allch_xs` vs `allch_y1h`).** The mean is about equal (+115.7 vs
  +113.5), but xs is more even: worst year +65 vs +48, without the 5 best days +66 vs +45, and
  the CI's lower bound is +53 vs +31. It is weaker one candle late (+55.7 vs +77.2). Neither is
  fragile.
- **Net-of-cost asymmetric target (`allch_cost`).** Worse on every robustness measure: 2022 is
  +2 bp, fragile, and the CI includes 0. Penalising over-forecasts makes the top 2% more
  conservative and drops the big 2022 winners. Rejected.
- **All 13 channels with a 1h point target** (`allch_y1h`, +113.5) sits between market (+96.8)
  and path_all (+152.0). The path quantile head, not the extra channels, carries most of
  path_all's edge.
- **5 seeds (`path_all5`).** Ranks first by the key (+154.2 vs +152.0), but 2024 drops from +74.8
  to +57.0, and without the best days it is +78 vs +85. This is seed noise of about ±10-20 bp a
  year, not a robustness gain. Its late 2024 is +5.6 bp (path_all +14.6).
- **6-monthly retraining (`path_all_h6`).** Mean +149.3, about path_all's level. 2022 falls from
  +162 to +115 and becomes fragile (-8 bp without its best trade). 2023 rises to +280, and the
  without-5-best-days and CI lower bound are the best of the single models (+86, +71). Late is
  weaker (+68.4 vs +88.8). The 2022H1 fold is the same model as path_all's 2022 fold (scores
  equal bit for bit), so the 2022 loss comes from the 2022H2 retrain.
- **GBM stacker.** Clearly worse than the CNNs on the years it covers: +82 / +5 on 2023-24 against
  path_all's +219 / +75. On 2024 it is below the scalars-only GBM (+29), and both go negative or
  flat at 0.6% in 2024. With fixed parameters and only 1-2 years of CNN-score history to fit
  on, it does not add to the CNN ranking. Rejected.
- **Ensembles.** Both qualify and neither is fragile.
  - ens_s3 is the most even variant: worst year +89, without the 5 best days +118 (the best of
    all rows), CI [+86, +221] (the highest lower bound), and 2024 +109 (the best 2024). Its weak
    spot is one candle late, +60.9; 2022 late is +16.5.
  - ens_top is close to path_all on everything (+151.2, 2024 +59 vs +75).

## Conclusion (2022-24 only)

- Nothing in S3 beats path_all clearly on the ranking key. path_all5 (+154.2), ens_top
  (+151.2), ens_s3 (+150.6) and path_all_h6 (+149.3) are all within the seed noise of path_all
  (+152.0).
- On robustness, **ens_s3** is the best result of the stage. It has the highest worst year
  (+89), the highest without-5-best-days (+118), the highest CI lower bound (+86) and the best
  2024 (+109), and it is not fragile. Its cost is execution: one candle late it keeps +60.9 bp,
  against path_all's +88.8.
- The rank-within-day loss evens out a 1h point model, but does not reach the path model.
- 6-monthly retraining and 5 seeds do not make path_all more robust.
- The net-of-cost asymmetric target and the GBM stacker are worse and are rejected.
- Candidates for S5: ens_s3 (robustness) and path_all / path_all5 (late entry), with ens_top as
  the path-only alternative.
