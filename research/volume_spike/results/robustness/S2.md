# S2: regime and execution (CNN robustness study)

Plan: `docs/plans/cnn-robustness.md`. Code: `s2_regime.py` (re-runnable: `score`, `timing`,
`report`). Ledger: `ledger.csv`, variants `S2:<scores>:<rule>`; cards in `cards/S2_*`; tables in
`S2_rules.csv`, `S2_timing.csv`. Only 2022-24 is shown; 2025-26 stays sealed.

## Setup

- Every rule is applied to both score sets with the S0 trading rule (pct >= 0.98 or ens3 mean
  >= 0.98, 5 equal slots, 1h hold, 0.1% + 0.05% per side, continuous production-engine book).
  The list of 14 rules x 2 score sets was fixed before any result; nothing exploratory was run.
- All filters use only the signal candle's close:
  - BTC flush: `btc_ret_4h <= -2% / -4%`, `btc_vol_mult >= 4 / 6` (seq2_meta).
  - Breadth: `spike_share` of `market.parquet` at the signal candle against the median (0.0889)
    and 75th percentile (0.1692) of the spike rows of 2020-21. The walk-forward scores start in
    2022, so "scored rows in 2020-21" are the spike rows of seq2_meta in 2020-21. Third gate: the
    number of *candidates* (score >= 0.98, per score set) on the same UTC day with open time at
    or before this candle (co-candidates of the same candle included) is >= 3.
  - Own performance: **two-pass**, not an engine hook. Pass 1 is the ungated book (same scores
    and rule); a candidate is allowed when the mean net return of the 20 most recent ungated
    trades with exit time <= its entry time is >= 0 (or fewer than 20 have closed). Why not a
    risk check on the real book: a gate that stops trading also stops new closed trades, so it
    could never reopen (a deadlock) unless it were fed paper trades, which is the same as the
    ungated shadow book. The two-pass version is causal (only trades closed before the entry),
    leaves `src/` untouched and keeps the engine golden tests valid. Limitation: the shadow
    history is the ungated book's, not the gated book's own.
  - Liquidity / tick: `qv_base >= 20k / 50k`; `tick_rel <= 0.1%` (events, joined on `event`).
- Execution: latency 0.05% / 0.1% per side (`Costs.latency`, charged on the market buy and on the
  market time exit); entry delay 2 candles. The card's "0.6%" and "late" stresses are then
  applied on top of the rule's own entry delay (late = delay 3 for `delay2`), but the 0.6% stress
  replaces the rule's costs, so for the two latency rows the 0.6% column does not include
  latency (it equals the baseline's). Read their cost robustness off the row itself.
- "Fragile": some 2022-24 year with positive bp is <= 0 after dropping that year's single best
  trade (computed from the book's trades in `s2_regime.py`).
- "Trades/yr" is the 2022-24 average of the yearly trades. "w/o 5 days": the 5 best entry days of
  2022-24 dropped. Verdict is the plan's Selection rule (criteria 1-4).

## Entry timing inside the spike candle (diagnostic, not in the ledger, not tradable)

The 717 of the 719 baseline path_all trades of 2022-24 whose 1m candles exist, re-priced on 1m
candles: buy at the open of the 1m candle at t+5m (the engine's entry), t+4m or t+3m (t is the
signal candle's open; the signal is only known at t+5m, so t+3m and t+4m are earlier than the
signal). Exit 60 minutes after each entry (the close of the 1m candle opening 59 minutes later).
Fee 0.1% + slippage 0.05% per side.

| Year | Trades | t+5m (engine entry) | t+4m | t+3m |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 268 | +194 | +81 | -51 |
| 2023 | 138 | +219 | +131 | +65 |
| 2024 | 311 | +77 | +6 | -83 |
| 2022-24 (pooled) | 717 | +148 | +58 | -42 |

Paired difference to the engine entry: t+4m -90 bp (t -6.3), t+3m -190 bp (t -9.1). Entering
earlier is worse in every year, as B4 found for the rule: the CNN picks keep falling through the
candle's last minutes and the close is the better price. (The 2022 figure differs from the
book's +162 because a 1m close at +60m is not the 5m exit close and a few huge LUNA trades move
a small sample; 2023 and 2024 match the book to 2 bp.) There is nothing to gain from trying to
front-run the candle close.

## Conclusion (2022-24 only)

- **BTC flush gates help most, in both score sets and in every year.** `btcvol>=6` lifts the mean
  from +152 to +291 (path_all) and +149 to +275 (ens3) with every year above the baseline's
  mean and 2024 at +225 / +235; it also removes ens3's fragility. `btc4h<=-2%` is nearly as
  good (+222 / +227) with more trades (153 / 124 a year). `btc4h<=-4%` has the highest mean
  (+300 / +356) but on 80 / 66 trades a year it depends on a few days (+81 / +93 without the 5
  best days) and its placebo p95 is positive (+38), so it is the least reliable. Cost is fewer
  trades (240 -> 139-162 a year for path_all), which matters for capital use.
- **Breadth gates help moderately.** `share>=p75` +222 / +204, `share>=p50` +208 / +182; the
  candidates-today gate adds little (+170 / +156). They overlap with the BTC gates (crash days).
- **Own-performance gate hurts path_all** (+40, 2022 -134 bp: it pauses trading after the
  early-2022 losing run and misses the rebound) and is neutral for ens3 (+161). Not recommended.
- **Liquidity/tick filters do nothing reliable.** qv>=20k/50k: path_all +159/+160 (baseline
  +152) but worse late (+71/+46) and qv>=50k is fragile; tick<=0.1% +143 (path_all), +182 (ens3).
- **Execution costs:** latency 0.05% / 0.1% per side lowers the mean by about 10 / 20 bp
  (path_all +142 / +131, ens3 +139 / +129); both still qualify. Entry two candles late hurts
  path_all (+95; 2023 +84, 2024 +7, fragile) while ens3 +164 is carried by 2022 (+361) with 2024
  at +6: the strategy's edge is gone within ten minutes of the signal in the calm years.
- **Caveat on selection:** 28 ledger rows were added here; the gates were chosen from a fixed
  list but they are conditioning on the same crash days that drive the baseline's profits, and
  the gates keep far fewer, more crash-concentrated trades (a handful of market-wide crash days
  carry much of the gain: btc4h<=-4% falls to +81 bp without its 5 best days).
  Treat the BTC gates as the S5 candidates, with the S5 deflated Sharpe and 2025-26 as the test.
