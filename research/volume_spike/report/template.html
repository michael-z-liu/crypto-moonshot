Volume Spike Casebook

Your 2019 idea, tested on six years of data: a sudden 5-minute volume spike comes before a jump, so buy it and take a small profit. This casebook goes through every method I tried, the ones that failed included. Each method gets a sketch of the rule, charts from the real results and a verdict, so you can check the work and pick what to build on.

All returns are net: after a 0.3% round trip (0.1% fee and 0.05% slippage on each side). 1 bp = 0.01%. A method has an edge only if its net return stays above zero in years it was not tuned on.

Measure

"Per signal day" averages each day's trades first, so a crash day with 40 signals counts once. It's the steadier view for the capitulation rules. The slot-limited portfolio results (5 positions at most) appear in the finalists section.

How every test works

Every method goes through the same pipeline. The features use only candles up to the spike candle's close, and the trade buys at the next candle's open. Nothing peeks ahead.

Where to take it

Caveats that apply to everything

  • Survivorship bias. The coins are today's top ~200. Coins that crashed and never came back (LUNA, FTT, many 2021 alts) are missing, which flatters every dip-buying result.
  • Crash-time fills. The backtest buys at the next open plus 0.05% slippage. On flush days spreads widen and Binance has throttled before. Real fills could be much worse.
  • Few independent bets. The capitulation rules trade on 20–70 days a year. One bad week can decide a year.
  • Many variants tried. Several hundred rule variants were tried on 2020–2024. The 2025–26 test is the only clean check, and it's where the edge shrank.
  • Test period reuse. The finalists' test was the first look at 2025–26. The gradient-boosting model and the CNN were fitted and chosen without it, but I had already seen that period's results, so it isn't a fully blind test for them.
