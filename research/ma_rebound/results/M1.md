# M1: MA grid, candidates, limit fills and labels

This milestone builds the event tables that every later step reads. It contains no model and no
outcome statistics: no fill rates, returns, win rates or exit reasons, for any year. 2025-26 is
built into the cache like every other year but stays sealed.

## What was built

Run from `research/ma_rebound/` with `uv run --group research python <script>`. Each step runs
one process per coin (8 workers, spawn), writes `cache/parts/<table>/<SYMBOL>.parquet` and then
combines the parts into `cache/<table>.parquet`. A run that is cut off picks up the coins still
missing (`--force` rebuilds everything, `--symbols` builds only the coins named).

| Script | Output (in `cache/`) | Rows | Wall time (582 coins) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `build_bars.py` | `parts/bars_<tf>/` (1h 18.5M, 4h 4.6M, 1d 0.79M, 1w 114k, 1M 27k bars) | 24.1M | 8 s |
| `build_candidates.py` | `candidates.parquet` | 2,616,383 | 59 s |
| `build_fills.py` | `fills.parquet`: one row per candidate x buffer x scenario | 7,966,514 | 35 s |
| `build_labels.py` | `labels.parquet`, `fwd.parquet` | 23.9M, 831k | 182 s |
| `check_events.py` | pass/fail lines | 76 checks | 86 s |
| `m1_counts.py` | `results/M1_counts.md` | | 1 s |

The shared logic is in `events.py` and the parallel runner in `runner.py`. The labels table row
count is given only as a total, because its size per year would show fill counts.

### Tables

- **bars_<tf>**: `idx` (period number), `t` (bar open), OHLC, `quote_volume`, and `L<n>`, the
  level in force *during* the bar, for every study and placebo length of that timeframe. The 1d
  table also has `atr_d` and `liq` in force during the day.
- **candidates**: `cid` (`SYMBOL-tf-n-l|t-YYYYMMDDHHMM`, the trigger candle's open), `family`
  (`limit` | `touch`), `placebo`, `symbol`, `tf`, `n`, `trigger_t` (trigger candle open, µs UTC),
  `decision_t` (its close), `L`, `u`, `atr_d`, `liq`, `close` and `low` of the trigger candle,
  `distance` = (close/L - 1)/u, `bars_since_touch` (completed bars since the last bar whose low
  reached its level, null if there was none since the level was defined), `slope10` = (L - level
  10 bars earlier)/L/u, and `year` (UTC year of the trigger).
- **fills**: `cid`, `buffer` (null for touch-market), `scenario` (`base` | `stress`), `status`
  (`filled` | `expired` | `censored`), `kind` (`maker` | `taker`), `fill_t` (open of the fill
  candle), `fill_len` (1m or 5m), `price` (raw), `level_at_fill`.
- **labels**: `cid`, `buffer`, `scenario`, `exit` (E1..E6), `status` (`done` | `censored`),
  `entry_t`, `entry_price` (raw), `entry_kind`, `exit_t`, `exit_price` (raw), `reason`
  (`take_profit` | `stop` | `time` | `series_end`), and `net`, the net return under that row's
  scenario's costs.
- **fwd**: `cid`, `buffer` (0.05, or null for touch-market), `fwd` (32 x float32), `mfe`, `mae`,
  for study MAs in the base scenario.

Combinations: study MAs get limit fills for buffers 0, 0.05u and 0.15u with E1..E6, and
touch-market with E1..E6. Placebo MAs get limit fills for buffer 0.05u with E1 and E3, and
touch-market with E1. Each is built in both scenarios.

## Definitions settled in M1

The brief already fixed the items marked (brief). The others are choices made here.

**Bars and levels**
1. A bar exists if it has at least one candle. 1h and 4h bars are built from 5m candles. 1d bars
   are built from 5m candles, plus the pre-2020 daily candles for days before the coin's first 5m
   day. 1w and 1M bars are built from the 1d bars, so the week and the month that straddle
   2020-01-01 combine both sources.
2. The level in force during bar i is the mean close of the n bars before it, counting only bars
   that have candles. It is null if fewer than n bars exist, or if those n bars span more than
   1.1 x n periods (brief). It is computed exactly: closes are summed as integers of 1e-8 (Binance
   prices have at most 8 decimals; checked on every close) and divided once. This way a level that
   equals a tick price compares as equal to it. Plain float means put a few of these ties on
   different sides in different implementations, and the direct-loop check found them.
3. `atr_d` in force during a day is the mean true range of the 14 daily bars before it, divided
   by the last daily close (brief). True range uses the previous existing bar's close. It is null
   with fewer than 15 bars, or if the 14 bars span more than 15.4 days.
4. `liq` (brief: the previous 30 complete UTC days) is the total quote volume of the 30 calendar
   days before the day, divided by 30. Days without candles count as zero. It is null within 30
   days of the coin's first daily bar.
5. `u = atr_d x sqrt(T in days)`, taken on the trigger candle's day and frozen for the whole
   trade (brief).

**Candidates**
6. "The last k completed bars" are the last k bars that have candles. Each must have a defined
   level and a low above it.
7. *Above* is evaluated after each 5m candle and includes that candle's own low. So a limit
   trigger candle always has its low above L. A candle that falls from the armed zone through L in
   one 5m bar gives no limit candidate; it gives a touch-market candidate.
8. A candle that is not *above*, a candle on a day without `atr_d`, and the trigger itself each
   disarm. The liquidity floor (>= $1M) is applied only to the trigger. The arm and trigger state
   runs regardless of liquidity, so a trigger that fails the floor still disarms.
9. Touch-market (brief): the first 5m candle of a bar whose low reaches L, after k bars above. It
   needs `atr_d` and liquidity at that candle. Entry is a market buy at the next 5m candle's open
   (a taker), and `level_at_fill` is the level in force during that entry candle.

**Limit fills**
10. The order is placed at the trigger candle's close (`decision_t`) and is valid for candles
    opening in [decision, decision + T/2). Its price is L x (1 + b u), where L is the level in
    force during each candle; u stays frozen (brief).
11. The candle sequence is the production `fill_path`: 1m candles where a 5m candle has all five,
    otherwise the 5m candle. The fill is the first candle whose low is at or below the limit
    (brief). It fills at the limit price even when the candle opens below the limit, or when
    re-pricing moves the limit above the market. Both cases are conservative.
12. Taker test: the trigger close is compared with the limit in force at the first candle at or
    after the decision. That is normally the decision candle itself. If the trigger was the last
    5m candle of its bar, the new bar's level applies. A taker fills at that candle's open.
13. Stress fill: the first candle whose low is at or below limit x (1 - 5 bp), at the limit
    price. Taker fills, and touch-market entries, are the same in both scenarios; only the costs
    differ.

**Exits**
14. Each (fill, exit) is resolved by the production `resolve_exit`. The coarse candles start at
    the 5m candle that holds the fill, with 1m candles wherever that 5m candle is complete.
    - The reference price (the open of the first candle) is the entry price.
    - For a maker fill, the fill candle keeps its real low and close, but its high is cut to
      max(entry, close). A stop inside the fill candle counts (stop first, conservative). A take
      profit inside it counts only if the close reaches the target.
    - 1m candles of that 5m candle before the fill are flat at the entry price, so they cannot
      trigger anything.
    - A taker or touch-market fill uses the real candle from its open.
    - Time limits run from the fill candle's open: `max_hold` is the exit's limit plus the
      fill's offset into its 5m candle.
15. Take profit is +x u from the raw entry price. The stop is the absolute price L_at_fill x
    (1 - x u), converted to a fraction of the entry price. If the stop is at or above the entry
    price (possible for touch-market and taker entries after a fast drop), the trade exits at
    once at the entry price, reason `stop`. If the stop is at or below zero (u > 2), there is no
    stop.
16. E6's limit is min(2T, 28 days) (brief). A time exit sells at the close of the last candle
    that opens before the limit, at the limit time. Where 1m candles are missing, a 5m candle
    opening before the limit can stop out at its close, up to 5 minutes after the limit.
17. A series has *ended* if its last 5m candle closed at least 2 days before the data end. This
    matches `delisted.txt` exactly: all 187 coins that stop early are delisted. An ended series
    closes at its last close, reason `series_end`. If a series that has not ended runs out of
    candles before an exit or fill is decided, the result is `censored` (null), never closed.
    240 coins' 5m candles end on 2026-10-31 23:55 and every coin's 1m candles end on
    2026-10-31, so the effective end for those is one day before `DATA_END`; past that, 5m
    candles stand in for 1m.
18. Net return = sell x (1 - fee) / (buy x (1 + fee)) - 1, with fee 0.1%.
    - Base: the buy is the raw price, plus 0.1% for a taker. The sell is the raw price, minus
      0.1% for a stop, time or series-end exit; take profits have no slippage.
    - Stress: 0.1% more on each side, applied to the stress fill.
19. Forward path: for each step i = 1..32, the close of the last candle that opens before
    fill + iT/32, as (close/entry - 1)/u. MFE and MAE are the highest high and lowest low of the
    candles opening in [fill, fill + T), in units of u, with the maker fill candle's high cut as
    in item 14. A path is null when it would need candles past the end of a series that has not
    ended.

## Checks (`check_events.py`)

All 76 checks pass (seed 20261113). Before the full run, the 10-coin subset also passed with
seeds 1, 2, 4 and 5.

- **Invariants** run on candidates of every year, and on fills and labels with a trigger before
  2025:
  - ids are unique; the decision is the trigger close; every candidate meets the liquidity floor;
    u = atr_d sqrt(T)
  - limit triggers are inside the zone with lows above L; touch candles reach L
  - fills come after the decision and within T/2; maker fills are at the limit; takers only when
    the close was at or below it; stress fills are never earlier than the base fill and imply one
  - no censoring before 2025; exits come after the entry and by the time limit; take profits and
    stops appear only on the exits that have them; the exit combinations are complete; forward
    paths exist only for study MAs
- **Truncation** covers BTCUSDT and five random coins, three random cut times each in
  2021-2024. A rebuild at the cut leaves unchanged everything that was decided before it: every
  completed bar, every level, `atr_d` and `liq` known at the cut (including those of the forming
  bar), every candidate up to the cut, every fill decided before it, every exit known before it,
  and every forward path complete before it. Everything still open at the cut is marked
  `censored`.
- **Direct loop**: two random study candidates per timeframe and family (20 in all), with
  triggers in 2020-2024. A plain Python loop over the raw parquet candles uses datetimes and
  reads nothing from `events.py` or `resolve_exit`. It reproduces the level, `atr_d`, `liq` and
  u; the coin's entire trigger list for that MA and family up to 2024; each buffer's base and
  stress fill (time, price, kind, level at the fill); and every exit (time, price, reason).

## Points for later milestones

- `year` is the trigger year, but the plan's holdout is by **fill time**. A candidate triggered
  late in December 2024 can fill in January 2025. M2 and later must filter on `fill_t` (or
  `entry_t`).
- The level's span rule looks only at the n bars themselves (as the brief states). After a long
  halt, a level can therefore be built from bars that are months old. The *above* condition
  (k bars above the level) rules out almost all such cases, but the rule does not.
- Stops at or above the entry (item 15) can only occur for touch-market and taker entries.
  How often this happens has not been looked at, because it is an outcome.
