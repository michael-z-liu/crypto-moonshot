# M4: walk-forward CNN, base model and gradient-boosted benchmark

This milestone builds the model inputs, the walk-forward training harness (with every option M5
needs), the base CNN with seeds 0-2 and a gradient-boosted benchmark, and scores them with the
M3 scorecard under the base rule. Everything shown here is 2022-24, or training diagnostics of
folds 2022-2025, which train and validate only on data before 2025. Fold 2026 trained and scored
silently: its log is in `results/sealed/m4/` (gitignored, not opened).

## What was built

Run from `research/ma_rebound/` with `uv run --group research python <script>`.

| Script | What it does | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `build_inputs.py` | Breadth table on the 5m grid, then the inputs of all 1,102,317 study candidates (both families) in memory maps under `cache/m4/` (8 processes, coin by coin, resumable). | 10 s breadth + 285 s inputs |
| `train.py` | Walk-forward harness, variant registry (`train.py list`), `run <variant>`; scores to `cache/m4/scores/<out>/`. | base: about 13 min for 5 folds x 3 seeds |
| `gbm.py` | `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` benchmark (`run`), seed check (`seeds`). | 2.1 min |
| `check_inputs.py` | 20 PASS/FAIL checks: truncation, breadth causality, invariants, float16 precision, fold boundaries, score coverage. | about 2.5 min |
| `m4_score.py` | Ledger rows and cards (`m4_cards.txt`), deciles, curves (`results/m4/`). | 14 s |

**Cache** (`cache/m4/`, 18.6 GB of the 20 GB budget; 58 GB were free before the build):

| File | Shape | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fine.npy` float16 | (1,102,317, 14, 288) | 8.9 GB |
| `coarse.npy` float16 | (1,102,317, 17, 256) | 9.6 GB |
| `scalars.npy`, `other_ma.npy` float64 | (n, 9), (n, 15) | 0.2 GB |
| `rows.parquet`, `market.npz` | row index (= `cache/m3/cands.parquet` order), breadth | 0.08 GB |

The memory maps are preallocated and each worker writes its coin's rows into them directly, so
the build never holds a second copy on disk.

## Inputs

Everything ends at the trigger candle's close (`decision_t`). Prices are `(price / L - 1) / u`,
with L and u the candidate's own.

- **Fine window** (288 x 5m, the base model uses the last 96), 14 channels:
  - coin: open, high, low, close, `log1p(quote volume)`, taker-buy share, validity mask;
  - BTC: open, high, low, close, `log1p(quote volume)`. These are BTC's last candles closing by
    the decision, as `(p / BTC close at the decision - 1) / u_BTC`, where `u_BTC` is BTC's
    `atr_d` on the trigger day x sqrt(T);
  - breadth: the liquid universe's equal-weight log return index relative to the trigger
    candle, and the share of liquid coins above their 1d SMA50.
- **Coarse window** (256 bars of the MA's timeframe, the base model uses the last 128), 17
  channels:
  - coin: OHLC, the level path `(L_bar / L - 1) / u`, `log1p(quote volume)`, bar mask, level
    mask;
  - BTC: OHLC, volume, mask;
  - breadth: index, share, mask (masked before 2020, where there is no 5m grid).

  The last bar is the forming bar as it stands at the decision: built from the 5m candles up to
  the trigger, or for 1w and 1M from the completed days of the bar plus the forming day.
  Pre-2020 daily candles count, through the M1 bars.
- **Volume** is stored as `log1p(qv)`. The harness subtracts the median over the valid positions
  of whatever window it slices, so "relative to its window median" holds for every window
  length.
- **Scalars:** timeframe (one-hot), log length, `distance`, `log1p(bars_since_touch)` and
  `slope10`, each with a null flag (nulls set to 0), log `atr_d`, log `liq`. 13 in all, z-scored
  on the fold's training rows. Other MAs: the signed distance `(close / L_k - 1) / u` to each of
  the 15 study levels, as `sign x log1p(|x|)` plus a null flag (30 more scalars).
- **Breadth:** a coin is in the liquid universe at a 5m candle when its `liq` that day is
  >= $1M. Its return counts when the previous candle is exactly 5 minutes earlier.

## Model, folds, training

- **Net** (`train.Net`), 140,577 parameters:
  - two towers, fine (12 channels x 96) and coarse (14 x 128), each with conv blocks
    5-5-pool-5-pool-3-3 (32, 32, 64, 64, 64 filters), batch norm and GELU, then
    mean / max / last pooling (42.6k and 42.9k parameters);
  - a scalar MLP 13 -> 32 -> 32 (1.5k);
  - a head of 416 -> 128 -> dropout 0.2 -> 1 (53.5k).
- **Normalisation:** per-channel mean and sd over the valid positions of up to 40k of the fold's
  training rows, z-scores clipped at +-10, masked positions zeroed. Mask channels are not
  scaled.
- **Training:** AdamW (lr 1e-3, weight decay 1e-4), batch 1024, Huber loss (delta 1) on
  `clip(E1 gross / u, -5, 5)`. Early stopping on the validation Spearman, patience 2, at most 12
  epochs, best epoch restored.
- **Folds** (rows: train / valid / test):

  | Fold | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Rows | 41,125 / 33,755 / 106,934 | 105,156 / 32,429 / 107,052 | 167,439 / 33,829 / 150,924 | 246,850 / 46,904 / 125,038 | sealed / sealed / 68,489 |

  Train rows are decided and resolved (E1 exit) before Y-1-07-01. Validation rows are decided in
  [Y-1-07-01, Y-01-01) and resolved before Y-01-01. Test rows are every limit candidate decided
  in Y, filled or not. Fold 2026's train and validation sizes are withheld because they count
  2025 fills.
- **Reproducibility:** MPS gave bit-identical predictions when the same fold and seed were rerun
  (fold 2022, seed 0, 2 epochs, 106,934 test predictions, largest difference 0.0). So a run is
  reproducible on this machine. It may not be across torch versions or other hardware.
- **Runtime:** epochs take 3 / 7 / 11 / 16 s on folds 2022 / 2023 / 2024 / 2025 (inputs
  preloaded in RAM as float16: 3.9 GB, 18 s to load). One (fold, seed) takes 0.25-1.0 min. The
  whole base run took about 13 minutes.

## Checks (`check_inputs.py`, all 20 PASS)

- **Truncation:**
  - setup: 78 candidates on 8 coins (BTC, ETH and 6 drawn with seed 20261113), both families,
    every timeframe (7-8 per family x timeframe), trigger years 2020-2026;
  - method: the coin's and BTC's candles are cut at the decision (only candles closed by then),
    the bars are rebuilt with M1's `build_bars`, and the breadth table is cut there;
  - result: fine and coarse windows (float16 bytes), scalars and other-MA distances are
    identical to the stored rows.
- **Breadth causality:** the breadth table rebuilt from all 582 coins cut at 2021-03-05 06:50 and
  at 2023-09-03 16:45 equals the stored table up to then, bit for bit. Together with the
  truncation check, the breadth channels also have no look-ahead.
- **Invariants:**
  - the last fine close and the forming bar's close equal `distance`;
  - the level path ends at 0;
  - the own-MA entry of the other-MA scalars equals `distance`;
  - at the last 5m candle of a bar, the forming aggregates equal the completed bar (BTC, ETH,
    LINK, every timeframe);
  - no NaN or infinity in the windows, and no value at the +-60,000 clip.
- **float16:** against float32 rebuilt for 3 coins, the largest error in what the model sees
  (z-score clipped at +-10) is 0.008 sd (fine) and 0.006 sd (coarse). The raw values can be
  large: QUICKUSDT's apparent 1000:1 redenomination puts its coarse prices near 3.5e4 u, where float16's
  absolute error is about 0.02 sd before the clip.
- **Folds**, for the CNN and the GBM:
  - every fold's train / valid / test set is recomputed from the M3 label tables independently
    of `train.py`, and equals the saved sets;
  - all train decisions and E1 exits are before the cut; validation is inside its half-year with
    exits before Y; test is inside Y;
  - the sets are disjoint;
  - every limit candidate decided in 2022-26 has a percentile in [0, 1] in each score table
    (558,437 per table).

  The same checks pass on the smoke runs of `path`, `bracket`, `tfgroup` and `touch`.

## Definitions settled in M4

1. **Row order.** Inputs are stored for all study candidates, in the order of
   `cache/m3/cands.parquet` (asserted at build time). Row index = `cand`.
2. **Window alignment.**
   - "Last 96 / 288 5m candles" means the last candles that exist, so gaps compress, as M1's
     bars do. The coarse window likewise counts bars that have candles.
   - BTC's fine window is BTC's own last candles closing by the decision, not candles aligned to
     the coin's.
3. **Volume.** The stored channel is `log1p(qv)`. The median is subtracted at load time for the
   sliced window (valid positions only).
4. **Breadth.**
   - Liquid means `liq` >= $1M that day.
   - The 5m return needs the previous candle exactly 5 minutes earlier.
   - "Above SMA50" is the close against the 1d L50 level in force; coins without a level are
     excluded from the share.
   - The coarse breadth is sampled at each bar's last 5m slot, and is masked before 2020.
5. **Label end.** The label end for purging is the E1 exit time. For `path` it is also fill + T;
   for `bracket`, also the E3 exit.
6. **Early stopping and validation.**
   - All variants early-stop on the Spearman correlation against the `ret` target, not their own
     target.
   - The CNN's Huber delta is 1 (u units).
   - The GBM uses squared error, because scikit-learn's histogram model has no Huber loss. The
     target is already clipped to +-5.
7. **Percentile.** The score is the fraction of the fold's training-row scores strictly below it,
   over all training rows. `tfgroup` computes it per group.
8. **Ledger names.** `cnn-base`, `cnn-base-seed<i>` and `gbm-base`. The run id is in `source`.

## Points for the master

- **Percentiles against filled training rows select fewer candidates than 20%.** The reference
  is the training rows (fills only), but the test set includes the unfilled candidates. Those
  score lower, so `>= 0.80` selects about 10-16% of the 2022-24 candidates (37,924 of 364,910
  for the seed mean). This is what the spec says. M5's threshold rules will have the same
  shift.
- **Early epochs win.** Most (fold, seed) runs peak at epoch 0-2. In fold 2025 all three seeds
  keep epoch 0, and the validation curves are noisy (seed spread of 19.8 pp in rank value). A
  lower learning rate, or averaging epochs, would be an M5-style change, so I left it alone.
- **GBM fold 2024 hit the 1,000-iteration cap.** Its validation curve was still rising, so a
  higher cap might change that fold.
- **The fold-check console output** showed fold 2026's train and validation row counts once
  during development (counts of 2025 fills, similar to a fill count). They went only to my
  terminal, never to a file or commit. The check now prints them as "sealed". The base run's
  total wall time (about 13 min) includes fold 2026, so in principle its time can be inferred;
  no epoch count, loss or correlation of it was seen.
- **QUICKUSDT's apparent 1000:1 redenomination** gives coarse prices of up to 3.5e4 u before the
  redenomination date. Clipping in normalisation makes it harmless for the model, but the M1
  candles treat it as a real move.
