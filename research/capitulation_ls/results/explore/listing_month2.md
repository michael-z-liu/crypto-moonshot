# Listing momentum after the first month (entries 30-90 days after listing)

Script: `x7_listing_month2.py` (helpers `x7_lib.py`). Grid CSV: `x7_listing_month2_grid.csv`;
calibrations `x7_calibration_listing_month2_{short,long}.csv`. In-sample exploration; choose on 2022-24,
read 2025-26. The full grid, picks, books, entry-day sweep and calibration tables are in the appendix.

**Verdict: fails the targets.** The state of a listing at day 30/60/90 (return since day 1, 30-day trend,
distance to the high since listing) carries no usable information for shorts, and the pre-declared pick
(D90, off-high <= -50%, 30 d) reverses out of period (+16.0% -> -1.7% per trade). What is left is a
broad, slow *decay of all young listings* in months 2-3: shorting every listing 45-75 days after listing
for 30 days earns about +6..+10% per trade (both periods positive), but that is below +10%, carries a weak
2023, needs 30-day holds, and alone makes +3%..+48% a year outside 2025. Longs: a small momentum effect
(30-day trend >= +30%, 7 d hold: +3..+5%, ~10 trades a year), far from the target.

## Setup

* Listings: `e12_listings.parquet` (397 spot listings 2021-01 .. 2026; first 5m candle = listing),
  hourly x3 matrices (582 spot symbols, perps by spot name). 376-383 listings per entry day pass the
  filters (spot 24h volume >= $500k); about 222-224 of them have an active perp for the short.
* Entry at the open of the hour listing + D days; features from closes up to the hour before:
  `since_d1` = close / close at listing + 24 h - 1 (distance to the first-day price; the first-hour open of
  Binance listings is unreliable), `r30` = 30-day return (since listing at D30), `off_high` = close /
  highest high since listing + 24 h - 1.
* Long = spot, 0.4% round trip. Short = perp, active (>= $50k in each of the last two closed hours,
  >= 24 h history), isolated 1x (+100% = stake lost), 0.3% + funding. Hourly exits, adverse side first.
* Books: `e25_combined.book`, 10 slots, $10k each year, stake <= 0.5% of 24h volume, one position per
  coin; LMC = L + M + C (2022+), the new sleeve with priority 2.0.

Pre-declared grid (252 rows): D {30, 60, 90} x side x filter x hold {72 h, 7 d, 30 d} x exit.
Short filters: all, since_d1 <= 0 / -50%, r30 <= 0 / -30%, off_high <= -50% / -75%; exits liq only,
TP 50% + stop 50%. Long filters: all, since_d1 >= 0 / +50%, r30 >= 0 / +30%, off_high >= -20% / -35%;
exits none, TP 50% + stop 30%. Pick = best `e22_24` (mean of per-year means) per side with >= 20 trades
in 2022-24. Added after seeing the grid (labelled post-hoc): books for six cells and an entry-day sweep.

## Calibration

* Short pick (pool = all D90 listings with a perp, signal = rank of -off_high): Spearman -0.047; the
  deciles go -0.036, +0.068, +0.026, -0.050, +0.137, +0.111, +0.161, +0.039, +0.010, **-0.181**: the
  deepest fallers are the worst decile (18% stopped). No monotone signal.
* Long pick (pool = all D60 listings, signal = rank of r30): Spearman +0.125; the bottom six deciles
  -0.006..-0.070, the top four +0.005..+0.042 (+0.028, +0.042 for the top two). A weak, real-looking slope,
  but the top decile is +4% per trade before slot competition.

## Why it failed

* **No signal in the features (shorts).** Calibration is flat to inverted (Spearman -0.05; the strongest
  "weak listing" decile earns -18% with 18% stopped). The filters only shrink n; averaged over holds and
  entry days they earn +1.6..+3.2% against +2.5% for all listings. Unlike month 1 (M: below the listing
  price -> +11..+37% a trade), by month 2-3 being far below the first-day price or the high no longer
  separates future fallers: most listings are already down, and the deepest ones bounce or squeeze.
* **Out-of-period reversal.** The rule chosen on 2022-24 (D90, off-high <= -50%) earns +16.0% there and
  -1.7% in 2025-26 (2025 book -26%, drawdown -60%). D90 as a whole flips from +9.7% to -2.3%; D30 flips the
  other way (-7.7% -> +3.5%). Which day "works" depends on the years.
* **The remaining edge is a slow, generic decay below the target.** Shorting all listings at days 45-75 for
  30 days earns +6..+10% per trade (stopped 4-6%, median giveback 0.46), but only with 30-day holds (72 h
  and 7 d earn +1..+2%), with 2023 near zero, and with ~35-45 eligible listings a year; alone the book makes
  +3..+48% outside 2025, far from +50% every year.
* **Longs: momentum is small and rare.** Listings with a +30% 30-day trend gain +3..+5% over the next week
  (about 10 a year); 30-day longs lose 6-9% as young coins keep decaying.

**Lead (not a strategy yet):** the D45-75 decay continues the M sleeve's first-month decline. Worth
testing whether M's own coins (weak listings) keep falling after M's 30-day exit, i.e. a longer M hold or a
second M leg, judged on the same 2022-24 -> 2025-26 split.
