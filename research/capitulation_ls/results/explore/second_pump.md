# Second pumps (pump-then-pump chains): short the exhaustion, or long the momentum?

Scripts: `x7_second_pump.py` (hourly event definition, 180-row grid), `x7_second_pump_f.py` (the F/P
5m-spike event definition and the d01 simulator, 54-row grid), helpers `x7_lib.py`. Grids:
`x7_second_pump_grid.csv`, `x7_second_pump_f_grid.csv`; calibrations `x7_calibration_second_pump*_*.csv`.
Everything is in-sample exploration; the only holdout logic is *choose on 2022-24, read 2025-26*.

**Verdict: fails, on both sides.** A second pump within 1-14 days is neither exhaustion (short) nor
confirmed momentum (long). Shorting a repeat pump is *worse* than shorting a first pump on both event
definitions, with or without the funding gate; every one of the 66 long configurations loses money.
No repeat-pump config gets near +10% per trade with a usable sample, and none improves the LMC book's
bp per trade.

## Setup

Two event definitions, because the result turned out to depend on it (see "Detection matters" below).

1. **Hourly** (`x7_second_pump.py`): pump = hourly spot close / close w hours earlier - 1 >= thr
   (x3 hourly matrices, 582 spot symbols), first trigger hour per coin, then a 24 h lock; spot 24h volume
   >= $1M. **Repeat** = another event of the same coin 24-336 h earlier (third and later pumps of a chain
   count too); **first** = no event in the previous 14 days. Entry at the next hour's open (perp for shorts,
   spot for longs). Exits on hourly bars, adverse side first, gaps through a stop fill at the open.
2. **F-def** (`x7_second_pump_f.py`): the P/F pool of d01/x6. feats.parquet 5m volume-spike rows with
   ret_4h >= 40%, perp active, qv_base >= 20k, 2021+, simulated with `sim.simulate` (5m then hourly perp
   paths to 7 days, spot paths to 72 h). Prior pump = a feats row with ret_4h or ret_24h >= 40% (any
   liquidity) or an hourly event (w 4/24 h, thr 40%) of the same coin 1-14 days earlier.
   Pool 2,877 rows; 824 of them have a prior pump.

Both: shorts on the perp, active (>= $50k traded in each of the last two closed hours), isolated 1x
(+100% = stake lost), 0.3% round trip, funding paid from the funding history; gate = last settled
funding rate >= 0 (shorts not crowded). Longs on spot, 0.4% round trip. One position per coin while held,
no slot limit in the grids. Books: `e25_combined.book`, 10 slots, $10k each year, stake <= 0.5% of the
instrument's 24h volume, one position per coin; LMC = L + M + C (2022+), new sleeve priority 2.0.
`e22_24` / `e25_26` = mean of the per-year mean net per trade.

Pre-declared grids (all rows are in the appendices):
* Hourly: (w, thr) in {(4h, 40%), (4h, 60%), (24h, 40%), (24h, 60%)} x hold {24h, 72h, 7d} x short exits
  {liq only, TP 30%, stop 50%, TP 30% + stop 50%} x gate {off, on} (96) + long exits {none, TP 30%,
  stop 20%, TP 30% + stop 20%} (48) + first-pump references with base exits (36) = 180 rows.
  Repeat events: 217 / 40 / 1,412 / 374 (2021-26).
* F-def: set {all, repeat, first} x {short: gate off/on x hold 24h/72h/7d x exit liq/TP 30% (36);
  long: hold 24h/72h x exit none/TP 30%/stop 20% (18)} = 54 rows.

## Calibration

* Hourly pool (repeat pumps with both pumps >= 20%; signal = rank of min(this pump, largest prior
  pump)): flat. Short 7d Spearman +0.007, deciles -0.036..+0.004 with the stopped share rising from 13% to
  25% at the top; long Spearman -0.098, the top decile is the worst (-0.034, 49% stopped); gated short
  +0.069, flat -0.020..+0.018. (Caveat: at 20% detection the trigger-hour size sits near 20%, so the
  signal mostly ranks the prior pump.)
* F-def pool (all pumps; signal = rank of the largest prior pump, 0 for first pumps; entered = repeat):
  gated short (72h, TP 30%) Spearman -0.018, the two largest-prior-pump deciles are the worst (-0.008 and
  -0.047, 9% and 14% stopped) while first-pump deciles earn +0.06..+0.15; ungated short Spearman -0.008;
  long Spearman -0.002, every decile -0.01..-0.10.

## Detection matters: the F edge is a property of the 5m spike rows

On the hourly definition, *first* pumps shorted with the gate for 72h earn only +1.0% (225 trades)
against F's +9.5% (143). The hourly simulator itself reproduces F: re-simulating F's own 143 rows on
hourly perp bars (entry at the next hour's open) gives +9.0%. The difference is the event set: of 203
hourly gated first/repeat 4h-40% shorts in 2022+, the 60 that coincide (`x7_detect_check.py`) (+-6 h) with an F row earn +12.6% /
+10.3% (first / repeat), the 143 that do not earn -2.4% / -15.6% with 14% liquidated; 82 of F's 143 rows
have no hourly event (intra-hour spikes that do not hold to the hourly close). "Price up 40% in 4 h" is not
the signal; the 5m volume-spike row is part of it. Worth knowing for any F variant.

## Why it failed

* **The hypothesis has the sign wrong for shorts.** A prior pump makes the short worse, not better:
  F-def gated 72h +3.7% repeat vs +8.0% first; ungated +0.6% vs +3.5%; hourly w24/40% gated -2.8% vs
  -0.4%. The stopped share is higher for repeats (F-def ungated 10% vs 6%, gated 8% vs 5%; hourly w24/60%
  7d with stop 50%: 30%) and the median trade gives back more of its best move (0.58 vs 0.45). Calibration
  is flat to inverted: the biggest prior pumps are the worst decile. A coin that pumps twice has real demand
  (or a squeezable short base) and keeps squeezing; it is not exhausted.
* **The funding gate does not rescue repeats.** It helps them as much as first pumps (F-def +0.6% ->
  +3.7% vs +3.5% -> +8.0%), and on hourly 4h pumps it keeps only 30 of 88 repeat shorts (66% had negative
  funding, against 56% of first pumps, 284 of 509: second pumps are shorted/crowded more often), leaving samples too small to read.
* **No momentum either.** All 66 long configs lose: spot pumps revert within 24-168 h after costs. Repeat
  pumps revert a little less than first pumps (F-def 24h -3.2% vs -7.3%; hourly w24/40% 72h -3.6% vs
  -7.0%) but never turn positive; with a 20% stop 52-71% of longs are stopped, win rates are 18-42% and the
  median giveback is 1.0. A 30% take profit is reached by about a third of longs, which does not cover the
  stopped two thirds.
* **The in-sample winners are year noise.** The best 2022-24 short (hourly w24/60%, stop 50%, 7d: +8.7%)
  reads -1.0% in 2025-26; the configs above +10% have n <= 18 with 0-2 trades before 2025. The F-def gated
  repeat short's +12.4% in 2022-24 rests on 3 trades in 2022 (+29.5%) against -4.7% in 2024.
