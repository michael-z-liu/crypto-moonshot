## Reading

The perp-only long trades do not make the book less dependent on its best flush days before
2024, because perp-only coins barely existed then: without each year's best long day, LMC+ and
LMC both fall to a worst year of +21% (2022). From 2024 on they add independent days: in 2026,
without its best long day, LMC+ still makes +87% (LMC +32%), and +79% without its best two
(LMC +29%). The +50% target in a year without a big flush still depends on enough flush days.
