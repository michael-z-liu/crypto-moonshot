## Verdict: a lead, not confirmed (in-sample, two years)

- 69 of 72 pre-declared configurations are positive: perp-only pumps keep drifting up on average
  in 2025-26 (the mirror of the failed shorts in `f_perp_only.md`, where 19% hit +100%).
- But it is a lottery: win rate 35-45%, median trade -14..-16%, the mean is carried by the 18%
  that double. Clustered by day, neither checked configuration excludes zero (all pumps, TP 100%,
  72h: +474 bp, CI -87..+983; funding < 0, TP 100%, stop 30%: +682 bp, CI -199..+1676).
- In the book the funding < 0 version looks attractive (2025 +790% -> +1096%, 2026 +111% ->
  +227%, drawdown -18%) and the all-pumps version adds drawdown (-48% in 2026). The funding < 0
  configuration is the best region of the grid, chosen after seeing it, and perp-only coins only
  exist in 2025-26, so this is not counted as an improvement. Worth paper-trading alongside LMC+.

## Why it is not confirmed

1. Two years of data (234 days), almost all perp-only coins being 2025-26 listings, in a period
   when such coins squeezed often; no earlier regime to test on.
2. Lottery payoff: the per-trade mean has a day-bootstrap interval that includes zero, and the
   median trade loses 14%. Below the +10% per trade target in every configuration (best +6.8%).
3. It competes with the long and listing sleeves for slots in 2024 (2024 +152% -> +130..+138%).
