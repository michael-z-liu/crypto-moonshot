# M4: combined model, pick rule and frozen spec (discovery walk-forward 2021-23)

Generated by `m4_report.py` from `results/m4_results.csv`, `results/m4_ledger.csv` and `results/spec.json`. Walk-forward: for each test year Y in 2021, 2022, 2023 the model is trained on the discovery entries whose 30-day window ended by Y-01-01 and scores year Y (2023 ends at D = 2023-12-01). Nothing from 2024 on is read. Rates and returns are percent, lifts and excesses percentage points (pp). `ret` is the net trade return (fee 0.1% a side, 0.1% slippage on market fills); *stress* adds 0.3% a side.

## The choice rule (fixed before the ranking was looked at)

1. Eligible: at least 100 book trades (10% stop) in each test year, and the stress mean net return per book trade (10% stop) above the same-day random books' 95th percentile in each year.
2. Highest worst-year stress mean net return per trade (10% stop).
3. Within 0.1 pp of the best: the simplest (transparent before lean before market before coin before all; no gate before gate; larger K).
4. A GBM pick must beat the best eligible transparent candidate by more than its seed spread (range over the three single-seed books of the worst-year metric), else the transparent rule is frozen.
5. If nothing is eligible: the same ranking over the candidates with at least 100 trades a year.

**Decision:** eligible; best by the rule: rule_liq_dd_nogate k10_q0.95.

## Run 1 (superseded, kept in the ledger)

Run 1 used the in-sample training scores as the percentile reference, as the brief suggested. A boosted model's in-sample scores are more extreme than its scores on new entries, and the models trained on 2020-21 scored most of 2022 above their training 90th percentile, so the q thresholds took 0 to a few hundred entries a year in one year and tens of thousands in another: no variant x rule reached 100 book trades in every year except two `gbm_lean` rules. Run 2 changes the reference to out-of-fold training scores (4 contiguous blocks, purged 30 days; `model.oof_scores`) and, after looking at run 1, adds two variants: `gbm_ranks` (within-day ranks only, because the raw factor levels carry the regime the models extrapolated badly into 2022) and `rule_liq_dd_nogate` (the transparent sort without the gate, because the gate at the training days' 60th percentile of BTC's 30-day return almost never opens in 2022, so no gated rule can reach 100 trades that year). The choice rule was not changed. These additions were made after seeing run 1's ranking, which is a forking path; the reveal on 2024-26 is the only clean test.

## Notes

- Seed spread `gbm_all`: the largest per-year AUC range over seeds is 0.0193.
- Seed spread `gbm_coin`: the largest per-year AUC range over seeds is 0.0091.
- Seed spread `gbm_lean`: the largest per-year AUC range over seeds is 0.0092.
- Market timing: pooled AUC of the market-only model 0.482 against 0.493 for all factors (pooled AUC mixes years, so it also rewards the year level).
- For `gbm_all`'s top 5%, -4.4 pp of the hit10 lift is *when* and +0.5 pp is *which*.
- Written after reading the run 2 tables (the numbers they cite are in the tables above).
- The GBM does not work out of sample here. Its walk-forward AUC is 0.45-0.57 a year and below 0.5 pooled, below the transparent rules (0.56-0.59). With one to three training years the market factors teach it the regime of the training years (2020-21 bull), and it scores most of 2022 high: `gbm_all`'s and `gbm_market`'s top 5% have negative *when*. The within-day view is better (+3 to +6 pp hit10 for the top 3-10 coins a day), but the two-factor rank sort does more (+7 to +10 pp). So rule 4 was never needed: no GBM rule was eligible.
- Which, not when: the frozen rule has no market gate and its lift is almost all *which* (+7.6 pp hit10 pooled, -0.2 pp *when*). The gated rules pick good days in 2021 and 2023 but trade on only two months of 2022, so they cannot meet 100 trades a year.
- Only the ungated transparent variant, added after run 1, is eligible (6 of 144 combinations, all `rule_liq_dd_nogate`). Its q = 0.95 beat q = 0.90 by 0.15 pp of the worst-year metric, just over the 0.1 pp tie margin, so q is not well determined; K = 10 and q >= 0.90 take nearly the same trades because the book fills its 10 slots.
- The result does not rest on 2021: in each year the picks' hit10 is 6-8 pp above the same-day random 95th percentile, and the stress mean net return per trade (10% stop) is +4.9% / +2.8% / +5.2% against random 95th percentiles of +3.1% / -0.2% / +4.4%. 2023 is the narrowest margin (+0.8 pp).
- Stress (0.3% extra a side) costs about 0.6 pp a trade and keeps every year positive. It does not model capacity: the rule buys the least liquid panel coins (still >= $1M daily volume), and fills there can be worse than 0.4% a side for larger books.
- Book returns compound realised equity over hundreds of trades a year, so the yearly and the continuous books (+1,920% in 2021; +18,600% continuous at the 10% stop) are mechanical and not a forecast; the same-day random books also compound to +270%. Drawdowns are on realised equity only, so they understate intra-trade losses. Compare with the random books and use the mean return per trade as the sober measure.
- Selection bias: the transparent rule's factors and the lean set came from M3 on all of 2020-23, the choice is the best of 144 noisy combinations, and two variants were added after run 1. Expect the confirm period to come in below the discovery band.
