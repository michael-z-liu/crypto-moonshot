## How much of this is luck

hit10 was shuffled within each day 20 times and the within-day pipeline rerun on every coin factor (900 factor-runs; `results/m3_shuffle.csv`). Under
the shuffle:

- 95th percentile of the top-decile hit10 excess: +0.3 pp; of the best-end excess (the ranking metric): +0.4 pp.
- mean number of coin factors (of 45) passing the within-day half of the stability test: 21.8; in the real data 26 pass it (26 pass the raw half, 20 pass both).
- of the top-20 coin factors above, 20 exceed the shuffled best-end 95th percentile.

The shuffle destroys the coin-level persistence (the same coin on neighbouring days has overlapping windows and similar outcomes), so it is a floor for how large an excess can look by chance, not a ceiling: real noise is wider. The market factors are not shuffled (only 1,400 days, strongly autocorrelated); for them read the bootstrap intervals and the stable flag, remembering that 20 factors were looked at. The stable flag itself is weak: positive in 3 of 4 years is passed by many null factors (see the count above).

## Observations

- Base hit10 by year: 2020 27.5%, 2021 31.1%, 2022 22.3%, 2023 24.5%; the year-to-year swing in the base rate is itself 8.8 pp, larger than most factor effects below.
- Largest market spread: `m_btc_ret30` at +13.4 pp (95% +5.7 to +20.5); 8 of 20 market factors are stable.
- 4 of 20 market factors have a spread interval that excludes zero.
- Largest within-day coin excess: `c_log_liq` (bottom decile) at +5.7 pp hit10, +6.9 pp hit_ns, +2.3 pp mean ret_10.
- Of the top-20 coin factors, 20 also raise hit_ns (they find rallies) and 0 raise hit10 only (they avoid stop-outs without finding more rallies).
- 20 of the top-20 have a bootstrap interval for the excess that excludes zero.
- 20 coin factors pass both stability halves; the shuffled null passes the within-day half for 21.8 of 45 on average, against 26 in the real data.
- Interaction: 4 of the 10 factors have a positive top-decile excess in all three market-state terciles.
- Redundancy: 9 linked groups at |rho| > 0.8, the largest with 15 factors, so the 65 factors are far fewer independent ideas.
