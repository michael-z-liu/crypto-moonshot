# Market gate on history: description, not evidence (M7)

**These tables describe the gate on 2020-26. They are not evidence, and nothing here
declares the gate confirmed or refuted.** The protocol (fixed 2026-11-14T22:30Z) says why:

> History (2020-23 and 2024-26) is description, not evidence: the factor was chosen after seeing that it led M3 on 2020-23, and 2024-26 has been opened.

The verdict will come from `forward.py` on entries after the data end (2026-11-02), once at
least 180 days of them have complete windows and arm B has at least 100 trades (`results/forward.md`).

**Gate.** Open on day `D` when BTC's last daily close is above the mean of its last 50 daily
closes (`m_btc_sma50 > 0`); closed otherwise, and when the factor is null. No tuning.
**Arm A**: every usable entry, gate open against closed (a null factor counts as closed).
**Arm B**: the frozen rule's picks (`results/spec.json`, unchanged) on gate-open days only, in
the same 10-slot book; positions open when the gate closes are held to their own exit.
Discovery arm B is the M4 walk-forward (2021-23, trained on earlier years only); confirm arm B
is M5's scoring (retrained each 1 January). The ungated rows reproduce M4 and M5 exactly (asserted).
Generated by `m7_gate.py` from `m7_gate.csv`.

The 2023 span is 11 months (discovery `D` ends 2023-12-01) and 2026 is 9 months (confirm `D` ends
2026-10-02). A gate that is open for a third of the days thins a rule that already trades a few
hundred times a year: the forward test needs 100 gated trades and 180 days of complete windows,
so it cannot read out before the end of May 2027 (210 days after 2026-11-02) even if the gate
is open all that time.
